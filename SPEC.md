# Bridgephone

Bridgephone is a mobile-first webapp that is used to keep track of games in brigdge tournaments. It is used by a bridge club to compare the scores of games. Based on this comparison a winner is decided.


## Bridge club

Bridge phone can have multiple bridge clubs. A site admin is able to create these.

A bridge club has members. These members can have the following roles:
 * Site manager
 * Player

These members all have access to bridgephone with through a unique email address. To begin with they can only login via google authentication.

# Tournament

A site manager starts a tournament for a bridge club.

A tournament has members and these members form teams of two people.  

A tournament has 24 games. When creating the tournament the teams and which games they will play with other teams are predefined.

Once the tournament starts every games gets a score, that can be entered by anyone of any team playing that game during the tournament.

Once everybody has entered 24 scores, the tournament is finished and the final ranking is visible to all members that took part in the tournament.
